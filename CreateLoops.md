# CSCI 240 - Supplemental Instruction
### Compound Conditionals
---

For the following questions, create a loop that achieves the desired effect. Loop type does not matter, but there are more effective choices for some questions. 

1. Find the sum of all numbers from $1$ to $100$.

2. Find $10!$ (Factorial means $10 \times 9 \times 8 \times \ldots \times 1$)

3. Output the word "Cat!" thirty times, with each word taking up its own line.

4. Find the product of every odd number from $1$ to $100$.

5. Output the word "Dog!" on odd lines, "Cat!" on even lines, for $30$ lines. 

>#### Bonus:
>Find every prime number between $1$ and $100$.
